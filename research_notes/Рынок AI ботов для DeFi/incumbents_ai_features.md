# AI Features of Incumbent Crypto Wallets, Exchanges and Analytics Platforms (state as of 22 Oct 2026)

Research note scope: what large incumbents already ship in AI-assisted crypto/DeFi, to separate commoditized features from remaining gaps for a new AI DeFi assistant bot. Dates are given explicitly. Items dated before 2026 are labelled **[OLDER]**. Source quality is flagged where coverage is secondary, sponsored, or aggregator-only.

Summary of the 2026 pattern, used throughout: almost every major incumbent shipped an "agent access layer" in Feb–Oct 2026, usually an MCP server and/or CLI plus an isolated sub-account or dedicated agent wallet with spend limits. Consumer-facing "AI assistant inside the app" products followed, mostly from exchanges (Binance AI, Bybit AI, Kraken AI, Coinbase Advisor).

## 1. Wallets: what AI/agent features exist (Phantom, MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet/Base App, Rabby, Trust Wallet, OKX Wallet, Binance Wallet, Rainbow, Zerion, Backpack)?

### Takeaway
In 2026 the big wallets did not mainly ship in-wallet chatbots. They shipped **agent wallets and MCP/CLI connectors** that let external LLMs (Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor, OpenClaw) hold a separate, policy-limited wallet or propose transactions for user approval. Examples: Phantom MCP (Feb 2026), Coinbase Agentic Wallets (Feb 2026), Trust Wallet Agent Kit (Mar 2026), OKX Agentic Wallet (Mar 2026), Zerion CLI (May 2026), Base MCP (May 2026), MetaMask Agent Wallet (Jun 2026), Binance Agentic Wallet (Aug 2026). Most are developer- or terminal-oriented, early access or "preview", and focused on swaps and perps. Rabby, Rainbow and Backpack show no first-party AI feature in the sources found.

### Cited Findings

**Phantom**
- Feb 18–19, 2026: Phantom launched an MCP server that lets AI agents swap tokens, sign transactions and manage addresses. It is compatible with Claude, OpenClaw and other MCP clients. Coverage mostly restates a Phantom X post (aggregator) — [MEXC News](https://www.mexc.com/news/745605)
- The official docs list these tools: wallet (balances, transfer, `solana_send`/`evm_send`, sign, `simulate`, `evm_allowance`, `price`, `pay`), swaps (`buy`), `wallet_rebalance` (portfolio rebalancing via swaps), and a full Hyperliquid perps toolset (open/close/cancel/leverage/deposit/withdraw). Chains: Solana and EVM, with Hyperliquid for perps; Sui is deprecated. Transfers use a "simulation-first" flow. Swaps via `buy`/`rebalance` are stated to be fee-free — [Phantom docs](https://docs.phantom.com/phantom-mcp-server)
- Agents get a **new dedicated wallet** on authentication, not the user's personal wallet, and it must be funded. One docs version says the server is "currently in preview and may change at any time" and that "Phantom makes no guarantees around anything your agent may do". It advises using a separate account for agent testing — [Phantom docs (resources page)](https://docs.phantom.com/resources/phantom-mcp-server). The current main docs page did not show a preview label when fetched, so the status is inconsistent across doc versions — [Phantom docs](https://docs.phantom.com/phantom-mcp-server)
- No consumer in-app AI assistant inside Phantom was found. The search found only external-agent tooling — [MEXC News](https://www.mexc.com/news/745605); [Blockeden (Mar 2026, super-app overview)](https://blockeden.xyz/de/blog/2026/03/07/phantom-wallet-super-app-evolution/)
- Scam protection is rule- and simulation-based, not LLM-based. Phantom's transaction simulation via Blowfish warns about drainers and malicious permissions. A third-party 2026 explainer notes "scam detection lag means new attack vectors succeed before blocklists update" and refers to ~15M Phantom users (secondary source) — [BYDFi explainer](https://www.bydfi.com/en-ae/cointalk/why-safe-phantom-wallet-still-puts-you-at-risk)

### Inferences
- **Commoditized at the wallet layer (as of Oct 2026):** an "agent wallet with spend limits and allow-lists", MCP/CLI access for Claude/ChatGPT/Codex, simulation before signing, and Blockaid/Blowfish-style threat scanning. A new bot cannot differentiate on "an LLM can sign transactions".
- Nearly all wallet agent products are **developer/terminal-first** (CLI, `npx`, Claude Code), early access, preview, or alpha. Mainstream consumers still lack a polished, mobile, chat-native DeFi agent inside these wallets. Trust Wallet's in-wallet AI for all users is only announced.
- Wallet agents focus on **swaps, transfers, perps (Hyperliquid) and DCA/limit orders**. Explicit LP/yield management appears only in MetaMask (LP provisioning) and Base MCP (Morpho/Moonwell lending protocols). Ongoing position management (rebalancing yields, health-factor monitoring) is not described anywhere.
- "Scam warnings" remain mostly deterministic (simulation plus blocklists), not LLM explanations. LLM-generated plain-language explanations of what a transaction does, and why it is risky, appear to be an under-served niche.

### Gaps
- No usage or adoption numbers (agents connected, volume) found for Phantom MCP, MetaMask Agent Wallet, TWAK, OKX Agentic Wallet, Zerion CLI or Binance Agentic Wallet.
- Could not confirm whether MetaMask Agent Wallet reached general availability, or the price of Beast Mode.
- Could not confirm whether Trust Wallet's in-wallet AI or its Agent Marketplace shipped.
- No evidence found either way on whether Coinbase's Base App has an in-app LLM assistant; the 2025 Base App rebrand and its XMTP agent-chat features were not re-verified.
- Rabby, Rainbow and Backpack may have small AI features not covered by press; their changelogs were not checked.

## 2. Exchanges: AI assistants and trading agents (Binance, OKX, Bybit, Coinbase, Bitget, Kraken, Robinhood, plus Gemini)

### Takeaway
2026 was the year of the "agentic exchange". Gemini (Apr 27), Robinhood (May 27 equities, then crypto in Jul), Coinbase (Jun 11), Binance (Agent OS Aug 20, AI Pro Oct 19), OKX (Agent Trade Kit Mar 10), Bitget (GetClaw, Apr) and Bybit (Bybit AI, Sep 9) all let LLM agents **execute trades** inside isolated sub-accounts with user-set limits and optional per-trade approval. Kraken is relaunching its app around a goal-based AI. Kraken AI and Coinbase Advisor stay advisory, with no auto-execution. All of this is **CEX-account-bound and venue-siloed**, and liability is pushed onto users.

- **Commoditized (do not build as core differentiators):** NL market Q&A and token research; whale/smart-money lookups; MCP data access; "LLM places a swap or perp order"; agent wallets with daily caps and allow-lists; isolated CEX sub-accounts; paper trading; Blockaid-style threat scans; 4-hourly market briefs.
- **Open opportunities:**
  - (a) A **venue-neutral DeFi copilot** that unifies self-custodied positions across chains and protocols (lending, LP, vaults, perps) and executes cross-chain intents. Incumbents are structurally conflicted: each pushes its own venue, wallet or liquidity.
  - (b) **Position-aware, proactive risk alerts**: liquidation and health factor, stablecoin depeg, protocol exploit, oracle anomaly, approval hygiene, concentration risk, with one-tap remediation proposals.
  - (c) **Yield strategy automation with guardrails**: rate monitoring and rebalancing across lending markets and LPs with simulation, gas/slippage-aware execution, and auditable logs.
  - (d) **Explainability**: plain-language "what this transaction does and why it is risky" before signing, and post-hoc PnL attribution.
  - (e) **Validated strategies**: backtests, simulation and transparent track records, where incumbents explicitly disclaim performance.
  - (f) **Chat-native consumer UX** (Telegram, mobile) for non-developers, since most agent wallets are CLI or early access.
  - (g) **Injection-resistant permission design**: no actions triggered by public content, session keys, per-protocol scopes, human-in-the-loop above thresholds.
- Incumbents will likely close some gaps quickly: Trust Wallet's announced in-wallet AI and marketplace, Kraken's relaunch, MetaMask GA, Coinbase spending limits. Differentiation should rest on cross-venue neutrality, DeFi-position depth and risk intelligence rather than basic execution.
